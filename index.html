Research

My research centers on world models and interactive video generation — learning generative simulators of dynamic environments — together with the reinforcement- and imitation-learning methods that make agents act well inside them. I'm interested in real-time, streaming, and long-horizon generation, and in learning from imperfect or third-person demonstrations.
